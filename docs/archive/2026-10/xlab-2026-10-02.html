Engine rules — src/domain/backtest/ (pure, as built)

Series in, trades + statistics + report out. No IO: the store, the run dir and Jev sit outside (src/application/backtest/).

Time integrity (PRD § 39)

  • Series are read as of t only: bars with close ≤ t, funding / ctx rows with t_ms ≤ t.
  • Every candidate and trade carries decided_at_ms and data_asof_ms; a candidate that read past its decision is dropped (future_data).
  • checks.rs: six worlds (1h; 4h; 1d; 15m across both 2026 spring-forward switches; 1h and 1d with a split) × four moves after t (next bar × 1.5; every later row changed; the next bar deleted; everything cut) — candidates, skips and per-arm outcomes at or before t unchanged.
tests decisions_at_or_before_t_ignore_what_comes_after, arms_at_or_before_t_ignore_what_comes_after, every_kind_reads_nothing_after_its_decision

Prices

  • The price at an instant is the close of the bar ending exactly there (BarSeries::close_at).
  • Missing ⇒ the candidate is skipped (missing_anchor · missing_entry · missing_price), never interpolated.
  • Only instants in [from, to) decide; series reach back / forward for anchors, lookbacks, features and exits.
engine.rs module table · § 6

Fills + costs

  • Entry and exit at the instant's close; cost per side = taker_fee_bps + half-spread + slippage_bps.
  • Half-spread: fixed · abdi_ranaldo (the last window_bars bars closed before the instant; none ⇒ the floor) · ctx (the latest ctx row ≤ t, else the fallback).
  • Costs and funding in bps of the entry notional (the exit's fee on the entry notional: ≤ 0.1 bps on a 5 % move).
fills.rs, costs.rs · § 6, § 11

P&L

  • gross bps = side × (exit / entry − 1) × 10⁴ — what a linear USD perp of a fixed size pays (the paper ledger books the same). Signals stay log moves.
  • net bps = gross − costs ± funding; USD = net bps × notional / 10⁴; a pair: each leg notional / 2, the trade's bps the legs' mean.
test gross_is_the_simple_return_of_a_linear_perp (review finding 6)

Funding

  • Σ over the hours in (entry, exit] of −side × rate_1h × 10⁴ bps; a row books for the hour nearest its t_ms (HL stamps a few ms late).
  • A hold hour without a row ⇒ funding_complete = false — counted, never guessed. funding = false (DEX tokens) ⇒ 0.
fills.rs funding_over

Share splits

  • Bars closed at or before at ÷ ratio (o / h / l / c), volume × ratio, trades kept; ctx prices too; funding never — before any decision.
  • A bar that opens before at and closes after it is dropped (KIOXIA's 1d bar of 2026-09-28: open 356.5 pre-split, close 114.12 post).
  • KIOXIA unadjusted: the fade booked +11 609 bps (log); adjusted +594.3 gross / +604.9 net.
tests a_bar_straddling_a_split_is_dropped_not_half_adjusted, a_split_inside_a_bar_never_fabricates_a_move · § 6

Entry liquidity

  • min_entry_trades: a candidate whose entry bar (the bar ending at the decision) counts fewer trades is skipped, thin_entry — decided at the decision instant, time-honest.
  • HL keeps a no-trade hour as a flat bar at the previous close (n = 0): 1 278 of xyz:DKNG's 4 561 hourly bars.
  • weekend_fade_liquid (≥ 50 trades): 625 thin_entry skips.
§ 5; skips.json of 20261001T182835Z-weekend_fade_liquid

Arms

  • research: every candidate at notional_usd, no caps (the shadow ledger's view); a plan whose horizon passes the end of the data is dropped (missing_exit), early exits included.
  • capped: a ledger on [risk] + [paper]; the candidates of one instant by descending |signal|; an admitted candidate whose exit has no price keeps its exposure until its exit instant; a refused entry is recorded with its rule.
  • Gate arms: rules = every decided candidate, jev = the taken ones (+ _capped).
tests a_fill_drop_never_frees_capacity, a_total_loss_halt_reads_equity_after_all_exits_of_an_instant

Statistics

  • t clustered by period (CR1); t_stat_iid kept for comparison (rule W: 2.93 vs 6.51).
  • 95 % CI: bootstrap over periods, B = 2 000, seeded splitmix64 (seed = 7) — reruns print the same numbers.
  • Sharpe per period × √(observed rate of periods with trades); max drawdown on realized equity once per exit instant (research: USD + bps of one trade's notional; capped: USD + % of cash).
  • Robustness: mean without the best 5 trades, share of P&L from the best 2 periods, per-instrument table.
  • Paired jev − rules CI only when each arm traded in ≥ 2 periods and ≥ 99 % of resamples hold both; calibration = equal-width bins of p, Brier = mean (p − win)².
stats.rs module table · tests summary_figures_by_hand, drawdown_is_measured_once_per_exit_instant, paired_difference_needs_each_arm_in_two_periods, sharpe_is_annualised_by_the_rate_of_periods_with_trades

Periods

  • weekend_window: the window's last trading day (e.g. 2026-04-02 before Good Friday); daily_window: the entry date (local); other kinds: the UTC day of entry.
  • A daily_window day whose anchor, entry and exit are out of order in UTC (a DST gap) is skipped with a note.
kinds.rs, weekend_fade::anchor_date · test a_daily_anchor_in_the_spring_forward_gap_never_follows_the_entry

Run guards

  • [backtest] max_candidates (50 000): a run past it stops in candidates — nothing simulated or written.
  • [backtest] keep_runs (100): older run dirs pruned on write; never the decision cache; keep-<run id> pins one.
  • The backtest tool refuses more than 50 000 candidates or candidate skips before any arm or file.
  • The always-in spec (0.01 bps hourly fade, 75 names): an error in under 2 s at 113 MB RSS (was 244 688 candidates, 392 MiB, 876 MB).
§ 6, § 14 · tests a_run_past_max_candidates_fails_before_any_arm, a_run_past_the_tool_limit_is_refused_before_any_file

  • Mean net +93.9 bps simple (the replay's log mean +95.5); the same 53 positive.

Holdout discipline (as built)

StepWhat happens
1 Split firstholdout = the last third; market_history reads end at the split (a skill rule — the tool has no split guard)
2 Tune in-samplebacktest with split and no holdout runs and shows the in-sample half only: a time split decides nothing at or after it (a from at or after it is refused); an instruments split leaves the listed ids' candidates out — no holdout trade is simulated or written
3 One readthe chosen variant + "holdout": true: both halves run; one line appended to backtests/holdout-reads.jsonl before the text shows it (an unrecordable read is refused); line 1 = HOLDOUT of <split> (out-of-sample) — no whole-window figure
4 Countholdout read #n for this spec (per spec_sha256) and the split's reads across every spec; from #2 on: "fitted to it: say so"
5 Rows{"run_id": "<run id>", "view": "periods"} (or instruments, trades, notes) — by run id, never a path; a split run's holdout rows only with holdout: true (counted, via = "rows")
6 Verdictpromising: first read, holdout mean > 0, CI excludes 0, ≥ 30 trades over ≥ 8 periods, not carried by ≤ 2 periods, placebo ≤ 0 · weak: mean > 0 but CI spans 0, or a few periods, or only after more than one read · no-go: mean ≤ 0 or edge < costs
