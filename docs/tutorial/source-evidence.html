Source evidence

A fact read from an approved source (SEC EDGAR, EU TED) becomes one source record: the fact plus where, when and under which terms it was read. Nothing is shortened or rounded, and a model's reading is never stored as a fact.

One record

The fact and its provenance

Every field the opportunity PRD asks of a factual observation (§5.2) is on the record. Confidence and contradictions need many records, so they are computed later, never stored on one.

Parse status

A failed parse still leaves a record

The fact is typed per source: sec_filing, ted_notice, or unparsed with a reason. Errors are listed per field, never dropped.

  • okokA typed fact, no errors.
  • partpartialA typed fact plus at least one field error.
  • errerrorAn unparsed fact plus at least one field error.
Enforced in code

Guarantees

  • Unknown keys are refused. At every level: the record, its fact, amounts, places, origin, parse errors.
  • An inference is never a fact. Inference (text, model, prompt sha256, generation, as-of) is its own type; no record field and no fact kind takes it.
  • Money is text as written. NativeAmount keeps 150000.00 as 150000.00 with an ISO 4217 code; a JSON number, 1e5 or 1,000 is refused. Nothing is converted here.
  • Places keep their role. Buyer, performance, legal or delivery, each in ISO 3166 or NUTS: never one folded field.
  • Identity is checked. validate recomputes the content hash and the record id; a tampered fact or id fails.
  • Ids stay whole. Accessions, CIKs with their leading zeros, procedure ids and every hash round-trip unchanged.
