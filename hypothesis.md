# Research Hypothesis: AI-Driven Drug Discovery Optimization

## Title
**AI-Accelerated Drug Repurposing for Rare Diseases: A Hypothesis-Driven Computational Approach**

## Abstract

This hypothesis proposes that artificial intelligence-driven drug repurposing can significantly reduce the time and cost of discovering treatments for rare diseases, which currently lack pharmaceutical industry investment due to limited market incentives.

## Background

Rare diseases affect approximately 400 million people worldwide, yet only 5% of the 7,000+ known rare diseases have approved treatments. Traditional drug discovery takes 10-15 years and costs $1-2 billion. Drug repurposing—finding new uses for existing approved drugs—offers a faster, cheaper alternative.

## Hypothesis

**We hypothesize that a multi-modal AI system combining:**
1. Large language models for literature mining
2. Graph neural networks for molecular interaction prediction
3. Causal inference for target-disease association

**Can identify repurposing candidates with >80% positive predictive value for rare disease indications.**

## Methodology

1. **Literature Mining**: Extract disease-drug associations from PubMed, clinical trials, and patents
2. **Molecular Modeling**: Predict binding affinities using structure-based models
3. **Network Analysis**: Map protein-protein interaction networks for pathway validation
4. **Clinical Validation**: Partner with academic centers for retrospective cohort analysis

## Expected Outcomes

- 50+ repurposing candidates for top 100 rare diseases within 12 months
- At least 10 candidates advancing to Phase II clinical trials
- 70% reduction in discovery-to-trial timeline vs. de novo drug development
